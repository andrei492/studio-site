Pack It! privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Pack It! app ("the app"), made by Cozy Box Games, uses, why, and the choices you have. Questions: studiosupport62@gmail.com.

No account, no personal details

You don't sign up or log in. The app never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Saved on your phone

Your level progress, stars, coins, settings, suitcase colors, daily challenge streak, hints and your ad consent answer are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. What you have bought is also kept by the store and RevenueCat (see Purchases), so it can be restored.

Ads

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with ads. Google also uses this information to prevent fraud and abuse. Ads appear as optional videos you choose to watch for a reward, and as full-screen ads between levels.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form when it starts, before any ads are requested. You can consent, refuse, or choose purposes one by one. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads. On iPhone, Apple also asks whether apps may track you. If you say no, you still get ads, just less relevant ones. See how Google uses information in ads.

Analytics and crash reports

The current version of the app does not send analytics or crash reports. When it does, it will work as follows.

To find levels that are too hard and to fix crashes, the app sends usage events (for example "level 12 finished in 40 seconds with 3 stars") and crash reports to Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, together with device information (model, operating system version) and a random app instance ID. These do not include your name or contact details. See Firebase privacy and security.

Purchases

In-app purchases (VIP subscriptions, Remove Ads, hint and coin packs) are handled by Google Play or the App Store; the app never sees your payment details. Subscriptions renew until you cancel them in Google Play or App Store settings. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt and a random user ID so that your purchases work and can be restored. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

App updates

When the app starts, it checks Expo's update service (EAS Update) for small fixes. This check sends the app's version, your phone's platform (Android or iOS), your IP address and a random install ID made by the app, so the right fix is delivered. It contains no personal details. See the Expo privacy policy.

Test versions of the app may not include all of these services yet. When they do, they work as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Change your ad consent answer at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy choices (shown where the consent form applies).
  • Delete the app to remove everything saved on your device.
  • Ask us to delete your data: see Delete your data below.

Delete your data

This section is for Pack It! Suitcase Puzzle (Android package com.studio.packit), made by the developer Cozy Box Games. You don't need an account to ask for your data to be deleted.

Delete what is on your phone

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → Pack It! → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes your progress, coins, settings, hints and ad consent answer straight away. We keep no copy of them.

Ask us to delete the rest

  1. Email studiosupport62@gmail.com with the subject "Pack It! data deletion".
  2. If you bought something, include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in your purchase receipt email or at play.google.com → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history). This lets us find your purchase records. If you never bought anything, there is nothing for us to look up: uninstalling is enough.
  3. We reply within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: the purchase records and random app user ID that RevenueCat holds for your app install. After this, "Restore purchases" can't bring those purchases back, so cancel any VIP subscription in Google Play first.
  • Kept by Google: Google Play keeps its own records of your orders and payments, as Google's policies and tax law require. We can't delete them. Ask Google at myaccount.google.com.
  • Ads data: Google AdMob controls the data it collects to show ads, and keeps it under Google's retention policy. Reset or delete your advertising ID (Android: Settings → Google → Ads) to break the link to your device.
  • We keep your deletion email for up to 12 months as a record that we handled the request, then delete it.

Sharing and keeping data

We don't sell personal information. Information goes only to the services named above, which keep it according to their own policies. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
