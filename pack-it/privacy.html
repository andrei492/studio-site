Pack It! privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Pack It! app ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. Questions: studiosupport62@gmail.com.

No account, no personal details

You don't sign up or log in. The app never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Saved on your phone

Your level progress, stars, coins, settings, suitcase colors, daily challenge streak and what you have bought are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the EU, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalized ads; on iPhone, Apple asks whether apps may track you. If you say no, you still get ads, just less relevant ones. See how Google uses information in ads.

Analytics and crash reports

To find levels that are too hard and to fix crashes, the app sends usage events (for example "level 12 finished in 40 seconds with 3 stars") and crash reports to Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, together with device information (model, operating system version) and a random app instance ID. These do not include your name or contact details. See Firebase privacy and security.

Purchases

In-app purchases (VIP, Remove Ads, hint and coin packs) are handled by the App Store or Google Play; the app never sees your payment details. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt and a random user ID so that your purchases work and can be restored. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Test versions of the app may not include all of these services yet. When they do, they work as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Change your ad consent choice from the app's settings once it is available there.
  • Delete the app to remove everything saved on your device.
  • Email us to ask about, or ask to delete, information the services above hold about your device. Include the app name and your device type.

Sharing and keeping data

We don't sell personal information. Information goes only to the services named above, which keep it according to their own policies. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
